A BOAT OF US ORIGINAL

BOAT OF US

2023 · Friendship · Comedy · Chaos · Ongoing

Two people meet and somehow discover they’ve been living suspiciously parallel lives. Same situations, same problems, same choices — sometimes apparently even the same food at the same damn time. What starts with “wait, SAME, this happened to me too” turns into secrets, endless conversations, unsolicited scoldings, questionable translations, inside jokes, and the kind of friendship where neither has to explain too much before the other already gets it.

Through the chaos, they become each other’s safe place — showing up, standing up for each other, calling each other out when needed, and somehow always finding themselves in the same boat.

Starring: Mahi & Abhi · Heartfelt · Chaotic · Comforting · Suspiciously Synchronized

A MESSAGE FROM
THE OTHER MAIN CHARACTER

DEAR MAHI,

I appreciate you because you have always been there for me, even when you had your own things to deal with. You understand me without me having to explain everything, you listen to me, stand up for me, and help me when I need it. I appreciate all the little things you do — the long conversations, the honest advice, the scolding when I mess up, and the way we always fix things together.

Most of all, I appreciate you for being someone I can trust and be completely myself with. One thing I hope you never forget is how special you are to me. No matter what happens or how busy life gets, I hope you always remember that you have someone who genuinely believes in you and wants to see you happy. You deserve all the good things coming your way, and I hope you never doubt your worth or the impact you have on the people who love you.

— ABHI
